Elk Rapids Film Festival

Our Story

How three friends, a projector, and a lake created something unforgettable.

The Origin Story

In the summer of 2014, three best friends had an idea. It wasn't a particularly good idea — at least, that's what their wives said.

The pitch was simple: spend an entire week in Elk Rapids, Michigan, watching movies together. Not at a real film festival. Not with industry people or red carpets. Just three guys, a projector, a stack of films, and the long golden evenings of a Michigan summer.

The wives were skeptical, to say the least. "This is your worst idea ever" The eye rolls were legendary. But the three friends pressed on, picked their films, and headed north to Elk Rapids — a small town on the shores of Elk Lake, where the sunsets last forever and the water is always calling.

That first year was magic. Something about watching great films in a place that beautiful, with people you love — it just worked. The conversations lasted longer than the credits. The debates about which film was best went late into the night. And when it was over, all three friends said the same thing: "Same time next year?"

The wives started coming. Then the kids. What began as the guys' dream became a family tradition — one that's now woven into the fabric of Michigan summers.

Years later, the Elk Rapids Film Festival isn't just a week of movies. It's a week of being fully present with the people who matter most. Phones down. Projector on. Lake shimmering in the background.

How It Works

Four steps. Sixteen years. One perfect tradition.

01

Theme Selection

Each year, we pick a creative theme that guides the entire festival. From yacht rock to the 80's — the theme sets the tone and sparks the debate about which films deserve a spot.

02

The Voting Process

Every June, family and friends nominate and vote on which films fit the theme. Campaigning is encouraged. Bribery is tolerated. Filibusters are legendary.

03

Festival Week

The week of July 4th. Seven sacred days of cinema in Elk Rapids. We don't just watch movies — we create experiences. Lakeside projections. Boat screenings. Theater takeovers.

04

Festival Winner

At the end of the week, we vote on the best film. The winner earns a permanent place in festival history. The debates about who should have won are half the fun.

Memorable Moments

The moments that made the festival legendary.

2010

The Beginning

Three friends, a projector, and a dream. The first Elk Rapids Film Festival was born in a living room overlooking Elk Lake. The wives were skeptical. The tradition was set.

2018

Jaws on the Lake

The most legendary screening in festival history. We set up a screen on the shore of Elk Lake and watched Jaws with our feet in the water. Every ripple got a scream. Nobody went swimming after. Pure, terrifying magic.

2019

A Decade of Cinema

Ten years in. We celebrated with a special anniversary screening and looked back at a decade of themes, debates, and late-night conversations about film. The wives admitted it wasn't such a terrible idea after all.

2020

The Year We Adapted

The year that tested every tradition. But the festival found a way — because some things are too important to let go.

2022

Yacht Rock on a Boat

We rented a boat in the Elk Rapids marina and screened yacht rock films while floating on the water. Hall & Oates never sounded better. Smooth sailing, smoother soundtracks, and the sunset did the rest.

2023

The Theater Takeover

We rented out an entire movie theater for a private screening. For one night, the Elk Rapids Film Festival went full Hollywood. Popcorn was on the house. The marquee was ours.

2026

The Journey Continues

Sixteen years strong. New themes. New films. Same friends. Same lake. The best is yet to come.
