Our Story

It started in 2010 with a simple idea between three best friends: spend a week every summer watching great films together in Elk Rapids, Michigan.

Our wives thought it was a terrible idea at first. A whole week dedicated to movies? But somewhere between the opening credits and the closing toasts, something special happened. What began as a guys' trip became a cherished family tradition — one that's now woven into the fabric of our summers on the lake.

Every July, we pick a theme, vote on the films, and spend the week from the 4th through the 11th immersed in cinema, conversation, and the kind of laughter that only comes from 16 years of shared memories.

Greatest Hits

Some moments become legendary. These are ours.

Jaws on the Lake

We set up a screen on the shore and watched Jaws with our feet in Elk Lake. Every ripple in the water got a scream. Nobody went swimming after.

Yacht Rock on the Water

We rented a boat in the marina and screened yacht rock films while floating. Smooth sailing, smoother soundtracks.

The Theater Takeover

We rented out an entire theater for a private screening. For one night, the Elk Rapids Film Festival went full Hollywood.

The Annual Vote

Democracy in action. Each year, the film selection is put to a vote. Campaigning, alliances, and the occasional filibuster are all fair game.

Our Traditions

01

The Theme

Every year has a theme that guides the film selections. From noir to sci-fi, comedy to adventure — the theme sets the tone for the entire week.

02

The Vote

Films are nominated and voted on democratically. Lobbying is encouraged. Bribery with snacks is tolerated.

03

The Screening

We don't just watch movies — we create experiences. Lakeside projections, boat screenings, theater rentals. Every film deserves the right setting.

04

The Week

July 4th through the 11th. Eight days. No exceptions. The festival is sacred time — phones down, projector on.
