How It All Started

In 2010, three best friends had an idea that their wives thought was terrible: spend an entire week watching movies together in Elk Rapids, Michigan.

But somewhere between the opening credits and the closing toasts, something unexpected happened. What started as a guys' trip became a cherished family tradition — one that's now woven into the fabric of sixteen Michigan summers.

Each July, we pick a theme, vote on films, and spend eight days immersed in cinema, conversation, and the kind of laughter that only comes from years of shared memories.
