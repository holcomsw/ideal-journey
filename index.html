The Legend of the Elk Rapids Film Festival

From Reels to Revelry

It began in 2014 with two dreamers—Scott Holcomb and Dan “Dusty” Graves—a bucket of audacity, and a projector aimed at a ceiling. They braved the mosquitoes of Elk Lake to screen the first-ever festival winner, Old School, birthing a cultural movement that skeptics said would never happen.

From those “boobs over the lake” formative years, the festival evolved into a decade-long legacy of cinema, friendship, and the relentless pursuit of “The Hedge”—the peak state of celebration with the Academy.

The Academy & The Evolution

The founding trio—Scott, Dan, and Robert “Roscoe” McLeod—built the festival on a foundation of Will Ferrell DNA, ensuring the “Prestige Worldwide” spirit lived in every screening. Over the years, the “Academy of Voters” expanded, leading to pivotal moments in our history:

  • The 2020 Coup d'état: Wives Erin, Bonnie, and Nancy seized programming control, introducing the “Tom Cruise” genre and more diverse curation.
  • The Legend of Jaws (2021): The festival hit its stride with an epic showing on a water trampoline, creating “future nostalgia” for the next generation.
  • Men 2 Boyz Reunion (2022): The legendary boy band reunited for the White on White album, proving that attire can, in fact, be “aggressive”.
  • The Solset Transition (2025): The festival expanded to its new sanctuary on Grand Traverse Bay, inaugurated with Ron Burgundy orange bathrobes and live sets by Todd Aldrich.

Our North Star

While we argue over movie rankings and the effectiveness of Sex Panther cologne, the festival has become a “North Star” for our children. It has taught them that joy comes from showing up fully, and that success is measured by moments so content you know the nostalgia will be sharp. Whether we are navigating “dissolving swim trunks” or avoiding neighborly brawls over yacht attire, the mission remains the same: reception, connection, and community.
